## The pattern to teach

1. Each wave automated the old work (hand coding, manual deployment, boilerplate) and raised the bar on what you have to show.
2. The jobs moved to people who could prove they solve the new problems.
3. AI is the same move: it lowers the cost of writing code, which raises the value of judgment, review, evals, and shipped proof. That's why this course starts with the portfolio.
