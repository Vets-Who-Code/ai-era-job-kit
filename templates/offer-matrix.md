## Four rules when the offer comes

1. **Ask for 48 hours:** "I'm excited. Can I have 48 hours to review?"
2. **Get it in writing.** Nothing counts until it's written down.
3. **Base first,** then sign-on, equity and title.
4. **Know your alternative** before you negotiate.

Don't give the first number. If base stalls, push on sign-on bonus, equity and title.
